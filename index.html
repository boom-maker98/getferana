Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you join.

What is Ferana?

Ferana is a data verification layer for your wearable. It cleans the signals from your device and builds a daily training plan around what your body is actually doing, with stress resilience built in.

Which wearables does it work with?

Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch, Garmin and Fitbit.

Is the biometric playground the real app?

No. The playground is an early v1.1 prototype that shows how the engine reads HRV, resting heart rate drift and sleep debt. Expect rough edges. The full app is still being built.

Is Ferana a medical device?

No. Ferana is a training tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

What happens to my data?

We collect only what we need: your email for the waitlist, and any feedback you choose to send. The playground does not store what you enter. We do not sell your personal data. Read the full privacy policy.
