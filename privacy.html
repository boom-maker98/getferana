Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

The short version

  • If you join the waitlist, we collect your email address.
  • The biometric playground does not store what you enter. If you send us feedback, we receive that.
  • We use this to run the waitlist, improve Ferana and talk to you about it.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time at FeranaOfficial@proton.me.

1. Who we are

Ferana is an early-stage project run by its founder. In this policy, "Ferana," "we" and "us" mean the people who run Ferana. You can reach us at FeranaOfficial@proton.me.

2. What we collect

  • Waitlist and newsletter. Your email address, and anything else you choose to give us when you sign up. Our email provider, beehiiv, collects and stores it for us.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback about the biometric playground, we receive what you write.
  • Messages. Anything you send us by email or direct message.

The playground does not store the values you enter (such as HRV, resting heart rate drift and sleep debt), and it does not connect to your Oura, Whoop, Apple Watch, Garmin or Fitbit account. You enter values yourself, and we do not keep them.

Our hosting providers automatically log basic technical information, such as IP addresses and browser type, to deliver and secure their services. We do not run our own analytics.

3. How we use it

  • To run the waitlist and send you updates about Ferana, including our newsletter.
  • To read your feedback and understand where the prototype is confusing or missing something.
  • To improve Ferana and develop new features.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To keep our services secure and to meet legal obligations.

4. Health-related information

Values you enter in the playground may relate to your health. The playground uses them only to show you results in that moment and does not store them.

Ferana is a training tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnose or treat any condition.

Before the full Ferana app collects any data from a wearable account, we will update this policy and ask for your clear permission first.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with companies that help us run Ferana, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • beehiiv, for the waitlist and newsletter.
  • Streamlit, which hosts the playground.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the company. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Cookies and analytics

We do not use advertising cookies or trackers. Our hosting and email providers may set cookies that are needed to deliver their services.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist email until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We keep feedback for as long as it helps us improve Ferana, and we delete it if you ask. We may keep limited records longer if the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link in any message.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing FeranaOfficial@proton.me.
  • Depending on where you live, such as California, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have additional rights, including objecting to or restricting how we use your data. We will respond within the time the law requires.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No website or service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Ferana is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Where your data is processed

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using Ferana you understand that your information may be transferred there.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Ferana grows. We will change the date at the top, and if the change is significant we will tell you by email.

13. Contact us

Questions or requests? Email FeranaOfficial@proton.me.
